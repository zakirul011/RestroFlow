Ask our team ↗
What does RestroFlow help manage?+

RestroFlow brings point of sale, menus, tables, kitchen orders, payments, expenses, and sales reporting into one restaurant management system.

Can I record bKash and Nagad payments?+

Yes. The system is designed to record cash, bKash, Nagad, and card payments against restaurant orders.

Which plan should I start with?+

Starter covers core operations for one location. Growth adds kitchen display and deeper reporting. Multi-location is for teams managing more than one restaurant.

Can JBD IT help us get set up?+

Get in touch with the JBD IT team to discuss onboarding and the right configuration for your restaurant.
